# GEO is not monitoring

There are already dozens of tools that will watch your AI visibility for you. They ping the
models on a schedule and show you a dashboard that says you were mentioned twelve percent of the
time. That is useful for exactly one thing: knowing you have a problem.

Knowing is not fixing. The gap the whole category leaves open is the work: generating the content,
the answer blocks, and the structured data that actually earn a citation, then shipping it. A
dashboard cannot do that for you.

## What actually moves the number

Based on the public research, the levers that matter are unglamorous and consistent:

- Answer-first content that states the point in the first sentence.
- Original statistics, quotes, and cited sources.
- Comparison and best-of pages in the exact shape an AI likes to quote.
- Being genuinely mentioned in places the models already trust.

Schema and an llms.txt file help at the margins for e-commerce and local, but on their own they do
not get a general B2B brand cited. Anyone selling you that is overclaiming.

## The honest loop

Diagnose where you are invisible. Fix it by doing the work. Then manage it at scale. Monitoring is
step zero. The value is in the steps after it.
