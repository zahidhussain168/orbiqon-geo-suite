# Why we report rates, not a yes or no

Ask an AI the same buyer question twice and you can get two different lists of brands. The output
is not deterministic. So any tool that tells you a flat yes or no about whether you are cited is,
at best, reporting a single coin flip.

We do it differently. We sample each prompt several times across every engine and report how often
you show up. Cited in three of five runs is a real, actionable signal. Cited, full stop, is not.

## Why this matters for trust

The number is only worth acting on if you believe it. A rate from repeated samples is honest about
the variance that is actually there. It is harder to game, and it does not overclaim.

It also keeps us honest about the fixes. We will never tell you a change guarantees a citation,
because nothing does. We can tell you a change moved your rate, and show the runs.

## What you get

- A visibility score from real sampled runs, not a single query.
- The competitors named instead of you, counted across runs.
- The exact prompts where you win and where you do not.
